What is AI? Decoding the terms
There are many terms being discussed today related to the topic. Which have you heard of? What do they actually mean?

Artificial Intelligence is simply a term for machines programmed to think and complete tasks like humans do.

Simple AI has existed for decades (think the enemies in your favorite video game)

Recent years have changed things:
Advancements in computer science have created new technologies (e.g. neural networks)
These technologies allowed us to create AI that learns from data rather than having to be programmed by humans (e.g. machine learning)
The most famous class of AI’s made this way imitate human speech (e.g. LLMs)

Examples of LLM’s?
ChatGPT by openAI, Gemini by Google, Claude by Anthropic, CoPilot by github

Current State of AI
Advancements in AI technology has been a focus of the news in recent years. We now have AI that are nearly as good as (or better than) humans at:
Having conversations
Playing specific games (stockfish with chess)
Automating tasks that were tedious to humans
Coding
Creating images/art


For many of these skills we used to think were unique to humans. While there are still a number of tasks AI are not capable of, the list is getting shorter every year.
