# Lesson 3 How Technology Impacts our Behaviour

Our first two lessons were about scientifically proven physical problems caused by technology (environmental damage, e-waste, etc)

This lesson is about more personal, more debatable impacts of technology, in particular, on how it’s affecting our behaviour and relationships.

**Conversation Challenge:**

- Students will be paired with a conversation partner and have a 5 minute, device-free conversation with them  
- Before pairing up, students think for 1 minute about how they might start that conversation (“What the HECCC”: Hobbies Entertainment Commonality Comedy Compliments)  
- Pairs may chat anywhere in the room


Note:  It’s perfectly okay if you run out of things to say\!  Learning to deal with an awkward silence is part of the challenge

---

For the remainder of the lesson, I’d like you to take up the challenge posed at the end of the video. Can you design a social media platform that would not cause the polarization we see currently?

With a partner, try to answer the following questions about your platform:

* How would you organize people? By IRL social connections (Facebook), common interests (Reddit), other?  
* What posts do people see? How much? How is it sorted? Likes? Dislikes?  
* How is it moderated? How do you deal with trolls?  
* How does discussion work?  
* Finally, what will you call the platform?
