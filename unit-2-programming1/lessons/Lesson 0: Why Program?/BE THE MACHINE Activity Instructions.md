**`BE THE MACHINE`**

Partner up with someone near you. If need be, you may form a group of 3\. Make sure you have a pencil and some paper.

You and your partner will alternate roles being the programmer and the computer. 

Sit back to back with your partner so that the computers are facing the back of the room and the programmers are facing the front. Make sure the computer in your group has the pencil and paper.

A word will be written on the board at the front of the room so that the programmers can see it. **The goal is to get the computers to draw that object within 2 minutes.**

| Rules |  |
| ----- | ----- |
| Programmer | Computer |
| May **not** look at the drawing May **not** describe the object **Can** give simple instructions like “draw a triangle”, “turn the page sideways”, “draw two small circles on top of each other” **Can’t** say things like “draw a head”, “It’s a duck” | May **not** talk Must only do what the programmer is instructing |

After two minutes, see how you did\! Then swap roles and try again.

*During the activity, think about what makes this difficult both as the computer and as the programmer.*
