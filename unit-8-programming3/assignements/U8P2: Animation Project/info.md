# **Animation Project**

**Use Python graphics to create an entertaining animated scene.  The scene should include:**

1. A detailed background (numerous objects that add interest or realism)

2. At least one moving composite object (i.e. an object made of more than one shape)

3. At least 3 episodes.  That means at least 3 for-loops that animate 3 separate unique actions. **WOW ME** marks may be awarded for 4 episodes or more.  
   For example, look at these projects from previous years:

   1. [*Archer and Apple*](https://codehs.com/sandbox/josephwaters/u8p2-example-1/run)  
      1. Archer pulls back on the bow  
      2. Arrow flies  
      3. Apple splits and falls

   2. [*Youtube Search*](https://codehs.com/sandbox/josephwaters/u8p2-example-2/run)   
      1. Blinking type cursor  
      2. Mouse movement  
      3. Optical illusion video

   3. [*Crabby Day*](https://codehs.com/sandbox/josephwaters/u8p2-example-3/run)  
      1. Waves on the beach  
      2. Crab crawling on the beach  
      3. Bird flying  
      4. Bird attack  
      5. Crab claws  
      6. Coconut drop  
   4. [*Frog on a Pogo Stick*](https://codehs.com/sandbox/josephwaters/u8p2-example-4/run)

   

4. At least one object that moves along a curved path, such as either:  
   1. a parabola (quadratic formula)  
   2. a wave (sin or cos)  
   3. a circle (sin and cos)

Here are some themes you could use:

* Outer space  
* Sports  
* Under the sea  
* Sky  
* Alien invasion  
* Volcanoes, tornados or other forces of nature  
* Use your imagination\!


All of your graphics must be made from Tkinter.  Imported images are not permitted.
