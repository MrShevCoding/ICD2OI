# Computer Art Assignment

Write a Python program that creates an artistic picture.  Your picture can be:

* A nature scene  
* An indoor scene  
* A city scene  
* An outer space or underwater scene   
* A character or animal  
* A sports scene  
* …Or anything else that inspires your artistic genius\!

Your picture must include at least 1 of every type of graphical object we have learned so far:

* Rectangles  
* Ovals  
* Lines  
* Polygons  
* Polygons with smoothing  
* Arcs  
* Text

It must also include **2** different random patterns.  Examples include:

* A crowd randomly placed of people  
* A field of randomly colored flowers  
* Debris from an explosion   
* Smoke  
* A city skyline (a line of buildings with random heights)  
* Anything else you can imagine\!

You may add stars (or other simple circle patterns such as snow, or rain drops, etc), but they **do not** count towards the randomness requirement because we have already done that together.  
    
Imported images are not permitted. You may also not submit any code you have created previously.

You may use gridlines to help you plan your picture, but do not include them in the final program that you turn in.

Reference notes to tkinter: https://anzeljg.github.io/rin2/book2/2405/docs/tkinter/create_polygon.html
