Mr. Waters is currently in a crisis! He spent the whole day remembering his student’s names and forgot his favourite numbers. His favourite numbers are any that are made of just the digits 4 or 9, or both. For example, 4, 999, or 94.

That's so many numbers to remember though! Can you help figure out how many there are?

For this challenge, you must make a program that takes as input one number, N, from the user. Then, it must output how many of Mr. Waters' favourite numbers exist between 1 and N inclusive.

Sample 1:
Input - 23
Output - 2
Explanation - There are 2 favourite numbers between 1 and 23. They are 4, 9.

Sample 2:
Input - 49
Output - 4
Explanation - There are 4 favourite numbers between 1 and 49. They are 4, 9, 44, and 49,
