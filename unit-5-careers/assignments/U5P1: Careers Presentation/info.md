# Careers Assignment

### **Purpose**

In this project, you will research and present to the class a career that uses computer science or computing technology. 

Doing this project will help you

* Learn about the range of exciting careers that are open to you in CS  
* Develop your presentation skills

### **Content of the presentation**

You will give a \< 4 minute presentation to the class on the career you chose.

**Example**:  Brain surgeon

During your presentation, you will:

1. Describe in detail 3-5 main duties of the job.

   * Example:  *“*First thing in the morning, the brain surgeon consults with patients to prepare them for tomorrow’s surgery.  Mid morning, she plans her operation on a cancer patient to remove a tumor from his auditory cortex.  After the surgery, just before going home, she reads online journal articles on recent breakthroughs in spinal repair surgery.  To stay licensed in her field, she is required to attend two conferences per year to keep up with current research in brain surgery.”

2. Tell us in detail how someone would use software or computer technology on the job. Include names of machines and software products, along with what they do.

   * Example:  “Brain surgeons use MRI machines and PET scan machines to help diagnose brain problems without having to cut open the patient.  After interviewing and examining a new patient, he also uses DoctorsAid 3.0 diagnosis software, which lets him enter the patient’s symptoms along with MRI results.  The software then suggests possible causes for the patient’s problem.  During surgery, she uses computer-guided lasers held by robot arms to make incisions in the brain tissue that are too small and too precise for a human to do by hand.”

3. Sales pitch \- share why this job is awesome. Tell us the most interesting things you learned about this job. Something cool and surprising \- **not** something like how much money they earn.

   *  Example:  “The coolest thing I learned about neurosurgeons is that they have to wear adult diapers during surgery.  This is because surgeries can last from 4-8 hours, and you can’t leave the patient at any time.  One, it would be dangerous to the patient, and two, you’d have to completely re-sterilize your body and clothing every time you went to the washroom.”

### 

### **Presentation style**

The ability to speak before an audience and to present yourself well is important in all careers. For this reason, you should invest considerable effort into making your presentation look and sound good.

### **Audience Behaviour**

Being an attentive, polite member of the audience is required for full marks. While presentations are being given, you are expected to pay attention to the speaker, and not be distracted by phones, computers, other work, or classmates.
