For this challenge, you must take the provided starter code, and use turtle to create a piece of pixel art.
(The Tetris Pieces are just examples which you may delete)

Your art must be at least 8 pixels by 8 pixels.

You may do your own unique art piece, or try to recreate something from the internet or a video game. If you are recreating someone else’s art, please include a link to an image of the original.

If you are looking for inspiration, old video games (particularly some on the Super Nintendo) have fantastic pixel art. To find some, you can either google some terms like “Super nintendo pixel art sprites”, or take a look at https://www.spriters-resource.com/

HINT 1: You don't need them, but loops could help a lot with this challenge!
HINT 2: If you are looking for more colour options, you can use Hex values (give it a Google). For example, instead of "Red" you could type "#f22749" for a specific shade of red.
